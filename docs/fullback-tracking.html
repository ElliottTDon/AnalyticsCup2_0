Full-back tracking

Rule

Tracked means the nearest outfield opponent is within 6 m, goal-side, and moving with the full back for 1 s (cosine ≥ 0.60, speed difference ≤ 2 m/s), on an overlap or underlap in the attacking half.

Sample

260 runs. 50 tracked, 210 not. About 19% fully tracked.

Receptions

Received 20.0% when tracked versus 32.9% when not. Effect −0.129, interval −0.250 to −0.015.

Shots and xthreat

Shots 16.0% versus 17.6%, and xthreat 0.0159 versus 0.0194. Both intervals include zero.

Use

Keep this for receptions, not shots.
