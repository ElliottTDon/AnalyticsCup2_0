Then: our work, in order

1. Event EDA — block, pressure, box (20 matches)

Shot rate by block height: low block about 18.5–18.7%, medium about 9.0–9.3%, high 6.0%. By on-ball pressure, shot rate runs from 2.2% with no pressure to 21.4% with very high pressure — read pressure as context, because dangerous attacks attract pressure. Shot phases have a smaller defensive box, about 148–166 m² smaller in the low block. Evidence: Event EDA, full EDA notebook, team shot zones.

2. Coupled two-graph test

Defence proximity graph + attack Delaunay graph, Hungarian marking and a supra-Laplacian, on organised blocks. After baselines (OOP width, length, area, nearest defender, on-ball pressure), only supra-Laplacian lambda2 remained: residual −0.013, interval [−0.022, −0.004]. Everything else was null. Verdict: not a KPI. Evidence: Coupled graphs, graph notebook, graph tool notes.
